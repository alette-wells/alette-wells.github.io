In summer 2024, I studied properties of Markoff mod $$p$$ points (i.e., solutions to the Markoff equation mod $$p$$). The motivating question for this project was whether all mod $$p$$ solutions admit integer lifts.

{% include gallery caption="A graph of the integral Markoff tree." %}

It is conjectured that the Markoff equation 
$$X^2 + Y^2 + Z^2 = 3XYZ$$ satisfies the special Diophantine property that every mod $$p$$ solution lifts to an integer solution. Progress towards this conjecture has been made by studying the connectedness of the graphs obtained from the action of the Vieta group on the nonzero mod $$p$$ solutions to the Markoff equation. In this paper, we use results on Pisano periods of the Fibonacci sequence to obtain explicit results on the connectedness of special points in this graph. Find the paper [on ArXiV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23401).
