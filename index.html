Welcome!

I am a second-year graduate student in mathematics at the University of Chicago, advised by Danny Calegari. My interests lie broadly in studying low-dimensional geometry and topology through structures that arise dynamically on spaces.

Math

Some of the phenomena I have studied in my first year include flows and foliations; complex dynamics arising from rational maps and expanding Thurston maps; and symplectic topology and almost complex structures.

Outreach

I enjoy communicating math to students and the broader public.
  • In summer 2026, I mentored three undergraduates for the UChicago REU.
  • In Autumn 2026, I helped to organize a booth on minimal surfaces and surfaces with boundary for the South Side Science Festival.
